📧 Email Signature — Bilal Cheema

Click copy, then paste into Gmail, Outlook or Hotmail signature settings

📌 Gmail

  1. Click Copy Signature
  2. Gmail → Settings ⚙️ → See all settings
  3. Scroll to Signature → Create new
  4. Click in the box → Paste (Cmd+V / Ctrl+V)
  5. Scroll down → Save Changes

📌 Outlook / Hotmail

  1. Click Copy Signature
  2. Outlook → Settings ⚙️ → View all settings
  3. Mail → Compose and reply
  4. Under Email Signature → Paste (Cmd+V / Ctrl+V)
  5. Click Save
